Live Roleplay Instructions: The Public Hearing

The simulated chat above mirrors the actual public hearings. Now it is your turn to negotiate SUP-22-3. Factions must lobby the decision-makers, and the Town Council must vote.

  • Step 1: Breakout & Strategy (10 mins) - Divide into your assigned roles. Factions: decide which Tertiary goal you will explicitly offer as a concession. Town Council: decide what metrics you need to survive the next election.
  • Step 2: The Lobbying Floor (15 mins) - Factions (AWS, Citizens, Business) formally pitch their demands to the Town Council. Factions may form coalitions to present joint proposals (e.g., AWS agrees to a CBA to win Business support).
  • Step 3: Council Deliberation (10 mins) - Factions step back. The Town Council deliberates publicly. They cross-examine the factions' pitches and negotiate the final conditions among themselves.
  • Step 4: The Roll Call Vote (5 mins) - The Mayor calls for a vote on the finalized SUP proposal. Majority rules.
  • Step 5: Compare & Debrief (10 mins) - Input the Council's final voted conditions into the Policy Docket on the left. View the "Result Matrix" to see where the Town ultimately landed. Did you achieve a "Sustainable Mandate" or a "Political Crisis"?
